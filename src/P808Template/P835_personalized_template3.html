Training (every 0 hour(s))

NOTE: New Instruction. This HIT is newly designed with updated instruction. Please carefully read and follow

The training section is identical to the Rating section, for some of the samples you might get a feedback:

In this experiment you will be rating the speech quality of a target speaker in sound samples involving speech in background noise. Each trial will include 1 audio sample which contains two parts separated by a beep sound:

  • Part 1 - target speaker segment: The first 3-5 secs of the audio clip contain the voice of our target speaker that we are interested in.
  • Part 2 - judgement segment: The rest of the audio clip is taken from a different conversation in which we aimed to improve the speech quality of that target speaker from the target speaker segment.

Your task is to recognize the target speaker from the first part and then give your opinion about the speech quality of that person in the judgement segment of audio clip. Within each trial you will give three ratings asked in three questions all regarding the same audio sample. Note that the target speaker may change from one trail to next.

For each trial, you will be instructed to:

  • recognize the target speaker by listening to the first 3-5 sec of audio clip which ends by a beep sound,
  • rate his/her speech quality in the rest of that audio clip considering following aspects:
    • attend only to the speech signal of the target speaker and rate how distorted his/her speech signal sounds to you in the second part.
    • attend only to the background (noise or other speakers) in the judgement segment and rate how noticeable or intrusive that background sounds to you.
    • attend to the overall quality (including the speech signal and the background) in the judgement segment and rate your opinion of the OVERALL QUALITY of the sample focusing on the target speaker for purposes of everyday speech communication.

Important examples:

Example 1:

The speech of the target speaker in the judgment segment is clear (Not distorted, score 5) with no noticeable background distortion (score 5). Excellent overall quality (score 5).

 

Example 2:

Although the background is very intrusive (1), the speech signal of target speaker is only slightly distorted (score 4). The overall quality is Bad (score 1) as the system failed to recognize the target speaker and filter the rest.

 

Example 3:

The speech signal of target speaker is completely removed from judgment segment (very distorted, score1 ). Although in judgment segment there is no background noise, but voice of another person. It is considered as a very intrusive background (score 1). The overall quality is Bad (score 1) as the system failed to recognize the target speaker and filter her out in the judgement segment.

 

Example 4:

The speech signal of target speaker is completely removed from judgment segment (very distorted, score1 ). The judgment segment does not contain any voice but slightly noticeable (score 4) background noise. The overall quality is Bad (score 1) as the system completely removed target speaker from the judgement segment.
